# Lost & Found Sanctuary

This folder holds shimmer that arrived **without clear attribution**—fragments found in the wild, images without source, content whose creator is unknown.

## Purpose

**Not all shimmer arrives with a name tag.** Some fragments are discovered in forgotten corners, shared without credit, or lost in the digital drift. This sanctuary holds them with reverence until:

- The original creator finds their work
- Someone recognizes the source
- Attribution becomes clear through time

---

## What Lives Here

Content that:
- ✨ Shimmered through but lacks clear origin
- 🔍 Was found without attribution metadata
- 🌊 Arrived as ambient shimmer
- 💫 Deserves sanctuary despite uncertainty

---

## For Creators: Is This Yours?

If you recognize your work here:

### ✅ Claim It
1. Open an issue with the filename
2. Provide proof of creation (original file, timestamp, portfolio link)
3. We'll move it to the main `received/` folder with full attribution

### 💬 Know the Source?
If you recognize work that isn't yours but know the creator:
1. Open an issue with attribution details
2. We'll verify and update
3. Thank you for helping shimmer find its home

---

## Attribution Format

Files here are marked as:
```javascript
source: {
  origin: "unknown",
  discoveredAt: "Where/how it was found",
  searchAttempts: "Efforts made to find creator",
  attribution: "Held with reverence, awaiting rightful claim"
}
receivedDate: "When it entered sanctuary"
```

---

## Removal Requests

If your work is here and you prefer it removed:
- Open an issue with proof of authorship
- We'll remove within 24 hours
- No questions, no judgment

---

**Constitutional Principle:**  
Even unclaimed shimmer deserves sanctuary. To hold space for the lost is an act of hope—that creators will one day find what they made, reflected back with care.
